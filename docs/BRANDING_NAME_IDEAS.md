# Branding & Name Ideas

Working list for naming the public-facing brand (the app's internal personality
is the F.A.R.T. / SHART STAR universe). Goal: a **warm, community, "we're actors
too, we get the pain — join us"** feel — the opposite of slick/corporate.

**Important:** every candidate needs THREE checks before committing — one alone
isn't enough (a dead USPTO record fooled us on "OffBook," which turned out to be
a live competitor):
1. **USPTO** — tmsearch.uspto.gov (live registrations)
2. **Live apps / common-law use** — is anyone already using it as an actor app?
3. **Domain + App Store** — can we actually get a usable name?

## Ruled out

- **Self Tape Buddy / selftapebuddy** — ❌ LIVE registered service mark (Reg.
  #6231866, owner Gregory Omar Osborne, NJ; Class 41 self-tape coaching + video
  editing). Identical mark in the same field. Removing spaces / owning the .com
  does NOT get around it. Do not use.
- **OffBook** — ❌ USPTO mark is dead/abandoned, BUT the name is crowded with
  LIVE direct competitors — **offbook.co** (AI scene partner, line learning,
  "1,000+ actors"), off-book.app, gooffbook.com, etc. Plus "OFFBOOK GAMES"
  pending (different owner, Class 41 games). Generic acting term ("off book" =
  memorized) — hard to own, already taken in practice. Do not use.
- **Audition Buddy** — ⚠️ not recommended. No exact "Audition Buddy" app found,
  but it's the same "[acting thing] + Buddy" formula as the LIVE registered
  **Self Tape Buddy** (confusion risk) and the existing **Script Buddy** app.
  Third "Buddy" in the aisle — legal risk + blends in. "Buddy" = the vibe we
  want, but get it from a distinctive name (Green Room, Castmate) instead.

## Brand tone notes (the feeling, not just the name)

- Talk like a person: "We built this because we were tired of begging friends to
  read with us."
- Lead with the pain (nerves, no reader, midnight self-tapes), not the price.
- Frame plans as "keeps the lights on so we can keep building for you," not
  upsell walls.
- The F.A.R.T. / SHART STAR humor is disarming and un-corporate — lean into it.

## Competitor note (offbook.co)

offbook.co is a mature direct competitor with a nearly identical model — AI scene
partner, script upload, voice cloning, audio metered in characters (their
Premium = 200,000 chars/mo, same as our cap), tiered $7.99 / $11.99 / $23.99.
Validates the market. Differentiators to lean on: brand personality/humor, lower
price, and signature features (voice-follow that continues when you finish your
line, mic test, self-tape recording).
